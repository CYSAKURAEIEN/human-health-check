# Basic Science

## 📋 Overview

This part collects the mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology and medicine that have most shaped human society: formulas, laws, properties, and what they can be used for. The aim: **if humanity has to rebuild technology from scratch, it should have enough theory in hand** and not have to grope its way from nothing.

The other parts explain "how" (how to smelt iron, how to make soap, how to build a house); this part explains "why". Once you understand the principles, you can work things out for yourself when you meet a situation the book does not cover.

## 📖 How to study

```
1. Read Measurement and scientific method first: measuring and testing
   are the foundation of everything else
2. In mathematics, master the first four sections first (counting,
   proportion, equations, geometry); that is enough for everyday work
3. Read Basics of biology and medicine all the way through: handwashing,
   clean water, vaccines and nutrition are the ideas that save the most lives
4. Look up physics and chemistry as you need them: mechanics for building,
   heat for heating and cooking, electromagnetism for generating power,
   chemical reactions for smelting, soap-making and brewing
5. Apply the formulas to real problems and work them through on paper;
   this is far more useful than memorising them
```
