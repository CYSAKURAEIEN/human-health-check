# Advanced Science and Engineering

## 📋 Overview

[Basic Science](../../../docs/08-基础科学知识/README.md) covers the principles everyone should know. This part is for those who want to go further, and explains **how to rebuild technology layer by layer**: first the overall roadmap, then the pillars that hold up industry — structures and materials, electric power, and mechanical power.

The content is roughly at the level of an introductory university science and engineering course, with the emphasis on principles, estimation methods and approaches that are realistic in practice.

### Chapters

1. [Technology Rebuilding Roadmap](../../../docs/09-进阶科学与工程/00-技术重建路线图.md) - the technology tree and its stages; key bottlenecks such as fuel, precision machining, sulphuric acid, glass and paper; lapping three plates against each other and bootstrapping the lathe; resources that can be salvaged from ruins; shortcuts you can take; what technology communities of different sizes can sustain; books and items to preserve first
2. [Engineering Mechanics and Materials](../../../docs/09-进阶科学与工程/01-工程力学与材料.md) - resolving forces and sling angles, moment balance, stress and strain and a table of material strengths, safety factors, buckling, beam bending and estimation, trusses, arches, suspension cables, foundations, the iron–carbon system and the principles of heat treatment, alloys, ceramics and refractories, concrete, machine elements
3. [Electrical Engineering and Electronics](../../../docs/09-进阶科学与工程/02-电气工程与电子学.md) - Kirchhoff's laws, capacitors and inductors, alternating current and three-phase power, generators and small-scale generating schemes, electric motors, transformers, why power is transmitted at high voltage, off-grid power systems, storage batteries, the telegraph and Morse code, radio principles, semiconductors and logic gates
4. [Thermodynamics and Engines](../../../docs/09-进阶科学与工程/03-热力学与动力机械.md) - the laws of thermodynamics and Carnot efficiency, properties of steam, boiler safety, steam engines and Stirling engines, internal combustion engines and alternative fuels, heat-transfer calculations and insulation, evaporative cooling and ice storage, heat pumps, water, wind and animal power, comparing energy sources
5. [Higher Mathematics](../../../docs/09-进阶科学与工程/04-高等数学.md) - limits, derivatives and maxima/minima, Newton's method, integration and numerical integration, Taylor series, differential equations (cooling, growth, oscillation, the SIR epidemic model), complex numbers, systems of linear equations and least squares, confidence intervals and hypothesis testing (chi-squared test, t-test)
6. [Advanced Chemistry](../../../docs/09-进阶科学与工程/05-化学进阶.md) - electron configuration and chemical bonds, enthalpy and Gibbs free energy (why burning lime needs 900°C), chemical equilibrium and Le Chatelier's principle, reaction rates and catalysis, electrode potentials and electrolysis, organic functional groups and reactions, dry distillation of wood and pine resin, flame tests and titration, the chemistry of making soda ash, sulphuric acid and other industrial chemicals
7. [Modern Physics](../../../docs/09-进阶科学与工程/06-现代物理.md) - wave–particle duality of light, atomic energy levels and spectra, key points of quantum mechanics, energy bands and semiconductors, lasers, relativity and satellite positioning, radioactive decay and half-life dating, radiation dose, the principles of nuclear fission and fusion, the four fundamental interactions and the universe
8. [Advanced Biomedicine](../../../docs/09-进阶科学与工程/07-生物医学进阶.md) - the central dogma and mutation, the division of labour in the immune system and types of vaccine, physiology of circulation, breathing, body fluids and blood sugar, pharmacology (therapeutic index, half-life and steady state, dosing by body weight, interactions), microscopes, aseptic technique and Gram staining, diagnostic reasoning, evidence-based medicine and randomised controlled trials, inflammation and wound healing
9. [Computing and Information](../../../docs/09-进阶科学与工程/08-计算与信息.md) - the abacus, slide rules and mathematical tables, number bases and two's complement, Boolean logic, adders and flip-flops, from relays to transistors, computer architecture and instruction execution, searching, sorting and algorithm efficiency, data structures, information entropy and compression, error detection and correction, principles of encryption, packet switching, the lifespan of digital records and preserving them on paper
10. [Advanced Celestial Navigation](../../../docs/09-进阶科学与工程/09-天文导航进阶.md) - celestial coordinates, the sidereal day, Polaris and the Southern Cross, finding north and south from a stick's shadow, magnetic declination, finding latitude from Polaris and the noon sun (the solar declination formula), finding longitude from the time difference and the equation of time, quadrants and sextants, dead reckoning, the gnomon and leap years and leap months, Kepler's laws and satellite orbits, solar and lunar eclipses

## ⚠️ Notes

- The calculations in this part are simplified, for understanding and estimation. Houses that people live in, bridges, lifting equipment, and pressure vessels such as boilers cause injury and death if they fail. Have them designed by professionals if you can; if you cannot, use a larger safety factor and test first
- Use the material on high voltage, boilers and toxic gases only once you understand the principles, and always follow the safety rules

## 📖 How to study

```
1. Read the Technology Rebuilding Roadmap first, to see the whole picture and the priorities
2. Lay the groundwork with Basic Science first (especially mathematics and physics)
3. Then read as needed: Engineering Mechanics for building houses and bridges,
   Electrical Engineering for generating power, Thermodynamics and Engines
   for steam engines, waterwheels, windmills and insulation
4. Theoretical tools: Higher Mathematics (engineering calculations and statistics
   for trials), Advanced Chemistry (industrial chemistry), Modern Physics
   (semiconductors, radiation), Advanced Biomedicine (pharmacology, evidence-based medicine)
5. Information and navigation: Computing and Information (algorithms, preserving
   knowledge), Advanced Celestial Navigation (position finding, calendars)
```
