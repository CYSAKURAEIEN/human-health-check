# Pharmaceuticals

> **Medicines after the end of the world: stockpiles, substitutes and the limits of what is possible**

This part answers two questions at once: which medicines **really can** be made at home in a collapse (disinfectants, Chinese herbal medicines),
and which **simply cannot** (antibiotics, chemically synthesised drugs). The second answer matters just as much: it stops you wasting
time and materials in a desperate situation on things that cannot succeed, or poisoning yourself by trusting a folk remedy.

## ⚠️ Extremely important warning

### 🚨 Conditions of use

**The content of this part is only for the following extreme situations:**

- ✓ Civilisation has completely collapsed and the medical system has broken down
- ✓ Commercial medicines cannot be obtained
- ✓ A life is in danger and there is no other choice
- ✓ You have basic chemical knowledge

**Wherever modern medical care is available:**
- ✗ Never make your own medicines!
- ✗ See a doctor at once and use proper medicines!
- ✗ Home-made medicines are extremely risky!

### ☠️ Risk warning

**The deadly risks of home-made medicines:**

1. **The dose cannot be controlled accurately** → poisoning, or no effect
2. **Purity cannot be guaranteed** → toxic side effects
3. **Sterile conditions are hard to achieve** → risk of infection
4. **Allergic reactions are hard to predict** → anaphylactic shock
5. **Raw materials are misidentified** → fatal poisoning

**The risk of death is real and high!**

## 🎯 Learning path

### Stage 1: basic preparation (compulsory)

1. **Learn basic chemistry** (1-2 weeks)
   - Acids, bases and salts
   - Simple organic chemistry
   - Dose calculation

2. **Master making disinfectants** (2-4 weeks)
   - Distilling alcohol
   - Making up tincture of iodine
   - Start with the simple things!

3. **Learn the basics of Chinese herbal medicine** (ongoing)
   - Plant identification
   - Processing methods
   - Classic formulas

### Stage 2: extension (optional)

1. **Organise your stockpile systematically** (ongoing)
   - Stockpiling and rotating antibiotics and wound-care supplies
   - See [Antibiotics – stockpiling advice](02-antibiotics.md#6-stockpiling-advice)

2. **Making and keeping natural antibacterials** (2-4 weeks)
   - Honey (first choice for external use), propolis tincture, tea tree oil
   - See [Antibiotics – natural antibacterial substances](02-antibiotics.md#3-natural-antibacterial-substances)

### ❌ Not on the learning path

- **Home-made antibiotics**: there is no reliable method, and this handbook does not give one (see section 1 of that chapter for why)
- **Chemically synthesised drugs**: they need a professional laboratory and are not feasible after a collapse

## 💡 Advice for beginners

### ✅ Recommended first steps

1. **Start with disinfectants**
   - Distilling alcohol is the simplest
   - Immediately useful
   - Lowest risk

2. **Learn Chinese herbal medicine**
   - Plenty of free raw material (wild)
   - Relatively safe
   - Effects tested over a thousand years

3. **Build up a store of herbs**
   - Collect and dry common medicinal herbs
   - Make simple Chinese patent medicines
   - Keep them for emergencies

### ❌ Things beginners must not do

1. ✗ **Do not try to make antibiotics**
   - There is no reliable method (it is not "hard"; it cannot be done)
   - Failure cannot be detected, and it delays real treatment
   - Wild moulds can produce toxins
   - What to do instead: stockpile in advance + natural antibacterials + prevent infection

2. ✗ **Do not start without knowledge**
   - Learn basic chemistry first
   - Understand the principles
   - Know the risks

3. ✗ **Do not use plants you have not confirmed**
   - Many poisonous plants look like medicinal plants
   - Eating the wrong one can kill!
   - Use a plant only when you are 100% sure

4. ✗ **Do not ignore the dose**
   - "Every medicine is partly a poison"
   - Too large a dose = poison
   - Better too little than too much

## ⚠️ Key safety points

### Toxic substances

**Highly toxic; never use or handle:**
- ☠️ White arsenic (arsenic trioxide)
- ☠️ Cyanides
- ☠️ Mercury and its compounds
- ☠️ Lead compounds
- ☠️ Certain plant alkaloids (strychnine, aconitine, etc.)

**Never use them, even if an "ancient formula" includes them!**

### Dealing with allergic reactions

**Common signs of drug allergy:**
- Rash, redness and swelling
- Difficulty breathing
- Nausea, vomiting
- Fainting

**First aid:**
1. Stop the medicine at once!
2. Mild (only a rash or itching): if you have an antihistamine (chlorphenamine (chlorpheniramine)), take it at once and watch for any worsening
3. Difficulty breathing, a tight throat, swollen lips or face, dizziness or fainting (anaphylactic shock): **call 120 at once**; if there is an adrenaline (epinephrine) auto-injector, inject it into the outer thigh at once; lie them flat with the legs raised (let them sit up if breathing is difficult)
4. If breathing and heartbeat stop: start CPR at once; see [Acute Symptoms - Allergic reactions](../01-first-aid-and-family-health/05-acute-symptoms.md#allergic-reactions)

**Prevention:**
- The first time you use a new medicine, test a small dose
- Watch for 30 minutes
- Use it normally only if there is no reaction

### Infection control

**Every pharmaceutical operation must include:**
- Washing and disinfecting your hands
- Boiling equipment to disinfect it (at least 15 minutes)
- A clean work area
- Aseptic technique as far as possible

**Especially for injectable medicines:**
- They must be strictly sterile!
- Otherwise it is better not to use them at all!

## 📖 Glossary

- **Dose**: the amount of medicine taken at one time (mg, g, mL)
- **Course of treatment**: how long the medicine is taken for
- **Median lethal dose (LD50)**: the dose that kills 50% of test animals
- **Therapeutic window**: the range between the effective dose and the toxic dose
- **Incompatibility**: combinations of medicines that must not be used together
- **Processing (*paozhi*)**: the preparation and processing of Chinese herbs
- **Extraction**: separating the active ingredients from the raw material
- **Purification**: removing impurities
- **Crystallisation**: a solid coming out of solution

## 🌍 Alternatives

**When you cannot make medicines at all:**

### Prevention first
- Clean drinking water (boiled)
- Personal hygiene
- Adequate nutrition
- Disinfect wounds promptly

### Physical treatments
- Fever: drink plenty of water, use fewer clothes and covers, sponge with lukewarm water (only as an aid)
- Pain: cold packs, warm packs, massage
- Inflammation: rest, raise the affected part

### Strengthening immunity
- Enough sleep
- A balanced diet
- Moderate exercise
- Sunlight (vitamin D)

### Natural food remedies
- Garlic, onion (antibacterial)
- Honey (antibacterial, eases coughs)
- Ginger (warms the stomach, promotes sweating)
- Lemon (vitamin C)

**Prevention >>> treatment!**
